# Welcome to Blog App

A simple application for discovering, sharing,
and organizing your favorite blogs.

## What can you do?

- Browse blogs shared by other users.
- Create and share your own blogs.
- Like your favorite articles.
- Search blogs by title.
- Create your personal reading list.
- Mark blogs as read.

## Get started

[Browse blogs](/blogs) to discover new articles.

[Register](/register) to create an account
and start sharing your favorite blogs.

Already have an account? [Login](/login).
